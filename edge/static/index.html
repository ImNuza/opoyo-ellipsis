What this is

OPOYO is a floor-vibration fall detector. The product is a piezo puck bonded to a slab. This build is the proxy: iPhones on the floor send user-acceleration and a sound-level number to a Mac dashboard.

How the pipe works

  1. Each phone samples CoreMotion user-acceleration at 50 Hz and microphone RMS as dB.
  2. A JSON packet goes over UDP to the Mac on port 9000. Audio samples never leave the phone. Only one dB number does.
  3. The Mac keeps up to five phones, keyed by a UUID created on first launch.
  4. This page shows the combined floor, then one screen per phone so a judge can see that each device is sending.

Formulas

User acceleration is gravity-removed, in g.

mag = sqrt(ax² + ay² + az²)

dB = 20 · log10(max(rms, 1e-8))

Combined magnitude at a tick is the maximum mag among phones that sent a packet in the last 2.5 s. Combined sound is the maximum dB. Axes are not averaged. Each phone has its own heading.

Identity

A phone is not identified by MAC address. iOS will not give that to the app. The stable key is a UUID stored on the phone. The card shows the model, a four-character short id, and a slot name Phone 1 … Phone 5.

Edge inference (this process, not the browser)

UDP packets land here. A 2 s window of magnitude is passed to a 1D CNN stub in edge/infer.py. The stub returns timestamp, is_fall, confidence. Every result is logged. The cloud is contacted only when is_fall and confidence ≥ ESCALATE_MIN_CONFIDENCE (0.90 in edge/app.py).

InferenceResult: inference_id, timestamp, node_id (Phone 1 … Phone 5), room (1–5), is_fall, confidence

FallEvent (cloud only): same fields plus threshold, and is_fall is always true.

Telegram

The cloud process owns the escalation ladder. At t+0 after a gated fall it Telegrams next of kin and the senior. Twilio is not on the live path; the adapter remains for a future voice plug-in.

  • t+0: Telegram next of kin (TELEGRAM_CHAT_ID_NEXT_OF_KIN) and Telegram senior (TELEGRAM_CHAT_ID_SENIOR) asking them to reply yes if they are okay.
  • Senior “yes” closes the case as all-clear. Otherwise wait 60 s, then Telegram secondary (TELEGRAM_CHAT_ID_SECONDARY), then CareLine at t+180.

Phone keep-awake

iOS will not stream CoreMotion or mic in the background. Streaming turns the idle timer off. A Keep screen awake toggle keeps it off after Stop. That is the Friday path: face-down, screen on.

Limits

  • This is a phone-on-tile proxy, not a bonded piezo.
  • Campus Wi-Fi often blocks phone-to-laptop UDP. Personal hotspot is the trusted demo network.
  • A sixth phone is dropped.
  • No labelled fall corpus. The live classifier is a stub that always returns no-fall.
  • Escalate threshold is calibrated in the field. Default 0.90.

Tweak

Threshold, window, hop, and cloud URL are constants in edge/app.py. Restart the edge process after changing them.

Code: edge/infer.py (stub), edge/gate.py (threshold), server/tree.py (ladder), server/notify.py (Telegram copy).
