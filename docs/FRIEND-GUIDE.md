# OPOYO — recording (send this to the team)

You record how the **floor shakes**. The phone sits on the floor. One action = one CSV. Send that file on Telegram.

---

## 1. Install the app

**Android**
1. Gilchris sends you `OPOYO.apk` on Telegram.
2. Tap the file.
3. If Android says “install unknown apps”, allow it for Telegram, then install.
4. Open **OPOYO**. Allow **microphone**.

**iPhone**
The APK will not work. Gilchris or Dewa must install the app from a **Mac** (Xcode, cable). After that, open **OPOYO**. Allow **microphone**.

Do not try to build from GitHub on the phone.

---

## 2. Room setup (do this once, then don’t change it)

- Hard floor only: **tile or vinyl**. Not a rug, not a bed, not a table.
- Phone **case off**.
- Phone **face-down** on the floor. Not in your hand, not in a pocket, not leaning on a wall.
- You stand about **1.5 m** from the phone. **Same distance for every take.** Do not move closer for a “better” hit.
- Quiet room. TV/speaker **off** unless the label is `tv`.
- Nobody else walking around during a take.

---

## 3. How to record one take

Do this the same way every time.

1. Open OPOYO. Pick the **label** that matches the action (list below). Wrong label = unusable file.
2. Tap **Record**. Screen stays on.
3. **Stand still 2 seconds.** Do not walk to the phone after you hit Record. Be in position first.
4. Do **exactly one** action.
5. **Stand still 2 seconds.**
6. Tap **Stop take**.
7. Tap **Send …csv** → **Telegram** → Gilchris.

**Do not rename the file.** The name starts with the label (`heeldrop_…csv`). That is how we know the class.

If anything went wrong (you talked, you dropped twice, you walked, you laughed, someone entered the room): **delete it and redo. Do not send it.**

---

## 4. What to record

Do **not jump**. A jump leaves the floor. That is a different signal. We are not recording real falls either (unsafe). Heel-drop and bag are the stand-ins for a heavy soft hit on the tile.

### Positives — 20 files

| Label | How | Count |
|---|---|---|
| `heeldrop` | Socks or barefoot. Rise on **toes**, drop onto **both heels**, **once**. Stay still. | 10 |
| `bag` | Backpack **8–12 kg**, drop **flat** from about **waist height**, **once**. Stay still. | 10 |

### Negatives — ~28 files

Do **not** smash a saucepan. Book is enough for a hard click. Optional metal: keys or a cheap lid you don’t care about, labelled `pan`.

| Label | How | Count |
|---|---|---|
| `book` | Hardcover, drop once onto the same floor. | 5 |
| `pan` | **Skip the cookware.** Optional: bunch of **keys** or a cheap metal **lid**, drop once. If you skip this, do 2 extra `book` instead. | 5 (or 0 + extra books) |
| `door` | Slam a door in the **same room**. Phone stays on the floor. | 8 |
| `walk` | Five **normal** paces past the phone. No stomping. | 8 |
| `quiet` | **15 seconds**, nobody moving, no talking. | 2 |
| `tv` | Optional. Speaker/TV **on**, **no** impact, 10 seconds. | 2 |

About **50 files**. Split across people if you want, but **same floor** and **same ~1.5 m**.

## 5. When you send the CSVs

In the Telegram message also write:

- floor: tile / vinyl / …
- room: e.g. kitchen
- distance: e.g. 1.5 m
- case: off (should be off)
- for `bag` / `book` / `pan`: what it was (e.g. backpack 10 kg, keys)

---

## 6. Don’t

- Jump, or fall on purpose
- Two drops in one recording
- Change distance between takes
- Phone on a rug / bed / in a case / in your hand
- TV on during heeldrop/bag/book
- Walk over to stop the recording until after the 2 s still (stop, then you may move)
- Invent new labels
- Rename files
- Send a take you already know is messy

Questions → Gilchris. If unsure, don’t record yet.
